The previous attempt (c01p) OOMed in the denoise forward pass (peak 24.16 of 24.56 GiB).

The change: an env-guarded (`LL_OFFLOAD_IDLE=1`) insert right after the prompt-encoding if/else in `CausalDiffusionInferencePipeline.inference()` that moves `self.text_encoder` and `self.vae` to CPU and empties the CUDA cache.

v1 of the insert was placed between `if use_cfg:` and its `else:`. The `else:` re-attached to the inserted `if`, so on the env=1 path `unconditional_dict` would never be assigned, and on the env=0 + guidance!=1.0 path CFG's unconditional dict would be silently nulled. It compiled and passed the naive regressions (their env did not exercise the difference).
