## Verdict

- Recommended (and now scenario-default) configuration: mc_band=0.03, mc=0.5,
  mc_est=corr, mc_edge=hard, mc_smooth=off -> first full [DSME] pass of the
  hotspot axis: 64.0% pos-drift removal at zero measured fidelity cost.
- The Astra refinements are implemented and kept as options, but they REGRESS on
  this scenario: with disjoint spectral supports (bump 73% < 0.03 cyc/px; balls
  >= 0.06) the sharp cut is optimal, phase corr on the low band is already
  robust, and smoothing lags the wander it should remove. Options retained for
  scenarios with overlapping bands (real video), where flat_top/smooth may win.
- Both offline axes of the plugin now pass: luminance (gain_field, 87-92%, all
  engines) and hotspot (64.0%, no fidelity cost, motion preserved).
- E1 metric floor stands as a recorded caveat: for corrections with large
  per-frame translation, hf_ssim vs any baseline is bounded by the shift
  magnitude (~0.94 at 1.5px) — graded hf_ref comparisons are only meaningful
  for corrections that keep the corrected content within the metric's band.
