`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
The dispersion kernel and the SSM baseline are the *same math* seen two ways: both are sums of damped complex exponentials. The wave arm **hand-structures** its poles with a physical dispersion relation `Ω=vx·kx+vy·ky+β|k|` (propagation direction/speed are learnable but constrained); the SSM **freely learns** its poles. The falsifiable experiment the reviews want is precisely: does the physical structure *beat* the free-form SSM on rollout horizon? If not, the "wave-field" framing adds nothing and you fold back to an SSM — a clean kill criterion.

## One real bug I caught and fixed (by inspection)
The spatial `--linear-pad` conv initially used `ifftshift` placement + `[0,n)` crop. Under zero-padding that silently turns a **symmetric** spatial blur into a **one-sided** filter (the "below/right" taps read zero-padded off-signal). Fixed to natural placement + center crop — a correct "same" linear convolution. Time causality is unaffected because a causal kernel's future taps are already zero.
