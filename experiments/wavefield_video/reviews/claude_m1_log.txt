## What I found (the load-bearing reality check)

The best local wave checkpoint is `demo_ckpts/model_wave_w1r4.pt` at **grid 16**, and there's no 64×64 wave model at all. But real rollouts already exist: `demo_out/seed_170001_wave/prediction/` is 256 frames of genuine autoregressive wave-model output (content = moving balls — the same domain M0 was tuned on).

The grid-16 fact drives everything: `mc_band=0.03` cyc/px sits **below the fundamental** (1/16 = 0.0625), so the r<0.03 inner band collapses to DC and `complex_mc` degenerates to plain `complex`. The positional correction that won at grid 64 is mathematically inexpressible at grid 16. So the **luminance/`magnitude`** axis (grid-agnostic) is the meaningful lever, and I feature it — while still running and reporting `complex_mc` honestly.
