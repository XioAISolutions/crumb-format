## Two caveats worth your eye (in the notes)
1. **`causal_time` alone isn't fully causal** — circular conv still wraps *past* taps to future frames. True causality needs `causal_time` **and** `linear_time`; that's why `--causal` sets both for wave. The flags stay independent as you specified.
2. **Causal sign convention** — I zero `dt<0` (the future-reaching taps under the actual convolution semantics), which is the opposite of the mislabeled comment in `kernels2d_smoke.py`. It's the version that makes the last-frame read-out genuinely autoregressive, and it's a one-character flip if you want the other convention.
