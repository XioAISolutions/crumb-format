**(1) `--resid-balanced`** (Opus leak #1) — in `motion_balanced_loss`, restricts the `0.25·resid` term to moving pixels. The insight that made this a 2-line change:

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
`MSE(pred-last, target-last)` is algebraically `MSE(pred, target)` — the `last` cancels — so the "residual" term was silently a **full-frame** MSE all along. That's exactly why it re-imported the copy-last shortcut and diluted ∝ `moving_frac` (~4× weaker at g32). Restricting it to `E[moving]` makes it grid-invariant.
`─────────────────────────────────────────────────`

**(2) `--motion-weighted`** (Astra soft variant) — replaces the hard moving mask with `w = clamp(|Δ|/(mean|Δ|+eps), 1, w_max)` applied to the motion term; static pixels clamp to `w=1`. Composable with (1). `--motion-w-max` default 10.

**(3) `--radius`/`--speed`** — passthrough to `data.make_clip_batch` (defaults = `RADIUS`/`SPEED`). Also scaled two dependents so the geometry control isn't confounded: collision `CONTACT` (`2r`) and the eval divergence thresholds (`div_thresh_px = radius`, `id_tol = 2·radius`).

**(4) `--eval-chunk`** (default 4) — `rollout_eval` forwards ≤`eval_chunk` seeds at once as independent rollouts, combining via seed-weighted MSE mean (exact) + seed-axis centroid concat. Auto-batch OOM handler now shrinks `eval_chunk` first (keeps all seeds → metric unchanged), dropping `eval_seeds` only at chunk=1.
