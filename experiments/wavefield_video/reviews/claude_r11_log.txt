`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- **Two paths that refuse to cross.** The strongest correctness guarantee here isn't a positive test — it's the *negative* one. A latent checkpoint's state keys are all `vp.*` (because `LatentCore` nests the `VideoPredictor` under `self.vp`), while a pixel checkpoint has bare `blocks.0.*`/`posemb.pt`. Each loader keys off exactly the prefix the other lacks, so `--latent` on a pixel ckpt and the pixel path on a latent ckpt both fail loudly instead of silently loading garbage. Smoke step (2) asserts this.
- **The AE reconstruction floor is a first-class metric, not a footnote.** Because every scored frame is a real `ae.decode(...)`, the reported MSE can never beat the AE's own `eval_recon_mse` — so I surface it directly in `metrics.json`. Without it, a reader could mistake the AE's lossy floor for predictor error.
