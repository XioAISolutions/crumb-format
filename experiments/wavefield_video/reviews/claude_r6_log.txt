`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- **Deinterleave is the hidden cost.** A torch `cfloat` tensor's `.real`/`.imag` are stride-2 *interleaved* views; Triton wants *planar* buffers. I moved the `.contiguous()` deinterleave **outside** the timed loop and pre-expanded broadcast operands (K, gate, lam, b) so the bench measures kernel-vs-kernel, not broadcast/copy overhead — otherwise the fused path looks artificially slow.
- **Where fusion actually pays:** the chain bench's FFTs dominate wall-clock, so the honest win shows up in the *isolated* `cplxmul`/`step` numbers (mul+gate+store in one pass, no intermediate DRAM round-trip), not the end-to-end chain — the notes flag this so the operator reads the JSON correctly.
`─────────────────────────────────────────────────`
