`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
The **whole-model** streaming equivalence (sanity check 8b) works because per-frame `step==forward` *composes through depth*. Block `i`'s streamed output sequence equals its windowed output sequence frame-by-frame, so block `i+1` receives an identical input stream in both paths — the recurrence's integrated history at layer `i+1` is built from exactly the frames it would have seen in `forward()`. This is why a T-frame stream reproduces the windowed prediction to `1e-3`, not just the single-mixer test.
